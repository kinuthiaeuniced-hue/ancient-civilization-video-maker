🚀 7. AI Video Generation Hub

Turn your documentary plan into a scene-by-scene AI video production workflow. This website prepares prompts and production instructions; the actual video clips are generated on the external AI platform you choose.

🎬 Runway
Good for generating and editing cinematic clips from text, images and existing video.
⚡ Kling AI
Useful for cinematic video, image-to-video and controlled multi-shot storytelling.
🌌 Luma
Useful for creating AI video from text prompts or source images.
✨ Pika
An additional option for short AI-generated visual sequences.
Choose a platform and scene, then generate a production-ready prompt.
How it works: Generate each documentary scene as a separate AI video clip, save the clips, then combine them with your narration, music and subtitles in the final editing stage. This is more practical for a 15–30 minute documentary than attempting to generate the entire film in one single request.

📦 Scene Production Checklist

1. Generate the scene prompt
2. Copy it into your chosen AI platform
3. Generate several short clips
4. Keep the best clips
5. Match clips with narration
6. Assemble all scenes into the final documentary
